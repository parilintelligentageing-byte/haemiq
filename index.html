04 · Questions

A few things people
have asked.

What does "Haemiq" mean?

Haem is the Greek root for blood — the foundation of haematology, the science of reading the body through its bloodwork. The IQ is the intelligence layer Haemiq adds on top. Together: blood, intelligently read.

When does Haemiq launch?

The MVP is targeting a soft launch in 8–12 weeks, starting with blood report analysis and smartwatch sync. AI meal planning and suggested tests follow shortly after. Waitlist members get access first.

Which smartwatches does it support?

Apple Health, Fitbit, Garmin, Google Fit, Whoop, and Samsung Health at launch — through a single integration so your data flows in regardless of which watch you wear.

Do I need a smartwatch to use Haemiq?

No. Haemiq is blood-first. You can use it with just blood reports and manual entry, or with both wearables and blood. The AI works with whatever signals you give it.

Is this medical advice?

No. Haemiq provides health insights and suggestions, not medical diagnoses. Everything the AI surfaces is meant to help you ask better questions at your next doctor's appointment — not replace one.

What happens to my data?

Your data is yours. Encrypted at rest, never sold, never shared. You can export everything or delete your account at any time. Full privacy policy goes live with the app.
