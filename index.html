Your data is everywhere. Your insight is nowhere.

Most people who get blood tests, wear smartwatches, and try to eat better are drowning in disconnected information. Their watch says one thing, their lab report says another, and their nutrition app knows nothing about either.

i.

Your watch sees patterns.

ii.

Your blood holds truth.

Inflammation, hormones, lipids, deficiencies. Decades of validated science. But you only see it twice a year.

iii.

Your meals fuel both.

What you eat affects every number on every report. But no app you own knows about either of them.

03 — Questions

A few things people have asked.

When does the app launch?

The MVP is targeting a soft launch in 8–12 weeks, starting with smartwatch sync and AI meal planning. Blood report analysis follows shortly after. Waitlist members get access first.

Which smartwatches will it support?

Apple Health, Fitbit, Garmin, Google Fit, Whoop, and Samsung Health at launch — through a single integration so your data flows in regardless of which watch you wear.

Do I need a smartwatch to use it?

No. You can use the app with manual entry only, or with just blood reports, or with both wearables and blood. The AI works with whatever signals you give it.

Is this medical advice?

No. Intelligent Ageing provides health insights and suggestions, not medical diagnoses. Everything the AI surfaces is meant to help you ask better questions at your next doctor's appointment — not replace one.

What happens to my data?

Your data is yours. Encrypted at rest, never sold, never shared. You can export everything or delete your account at any time. Privacy policy goes live with the app.
